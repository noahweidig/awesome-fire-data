# Insect and Disease Detection Survey (IDS)

Aerial survey polygons of tree damage from insects, disease and other agents.

[:lucide-download: Access data](https://data.fs.usda.gov/geodata/edw/datasets.php){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
[:lucide-book-open: Documentation](https://apps.fs.usda.gov/arcx/rest/services/EDW/EDW_InsectandDiseaseSurvey_01/MapServer){ .md-button }

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| :lucide-building-2:{ title="Who publishes the data" } Provider | USDA Forest Service Forest Health Protection |

!!! warning "Caveat"

    Aerial sketch mapping is subjective and survey coverage varies by year and region.
