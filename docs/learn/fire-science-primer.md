# Fire science primer

Fire behavior depends on three inputs: fuel, weather and terrain. Most fire datasets describe one of them or a result of them. See [Fire triangles](fire-triangles.md) for the three triangles at different scales.

Fuel
:   Vegetation that can burn, described by type, load, moisture and structure. See [Fuels, vegetation & terrain](../datasets/fuels-terrain/index.md).

Weather and danger indices
:   Wind, temperature, humidity and drought combine into indices such as the FWI and NFDRS. See [Weather, climate & danger](../datasets/weather-danger/index.md).

Fire radiative power
:   FRP measures the radiant heat of a burning pixel in megawatts. Active fire products report it.

Burn severity
:   Ecological change caused by fire, often mapped with differenced spectral indices from before and after imagery. See [MTBS](../datasets/perimeters/mtbs.md).

Wildland-urban interface
:   The WUI is where structures meet wildland fuel. It drives risk to people. See [Risk, WUI & management](../datasets/risk-management/index.md).
