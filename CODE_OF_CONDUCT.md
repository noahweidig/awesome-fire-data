# Code of Conduct

This project is a shared reference for fire science and management. Be respectful and keep discussion on the data.

## Expected behavior

- Be courteous and assume good faith.
- Give specific, sourced feedback on entries.
- Accept corrections to your contributions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Spam or promotion unrelated to fire data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and block users who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through [noahweidig.com](https://noahweidig.com) or open an issue that avoids sensitive details.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
